## 1. Overview & Scope

This specification standard defines the physical, mechanical, and biodegradation benchmarks of industrial and retail-grade **plant-based knitted viscose (regenerated cellulose) and PLA-viscose cleaning fabrics**. Engineered as a reusable, high-absorbency alternative to petroleum-derived synthetic microfiber (polyester/polyamide), these knitted textiles serve EU/US retail private-label programs, Japanese kitchenware brands, and commercial sanitation supply chains.

* **Primary Fiber Architecture**: Regenerated Wood/Plant Cellulose (Viscose/Rayon) and Biodegradable PLA-Viscose Blends
* **Knitting Technology**: High-gauge Circular Weft Knitting (35 in-house machines)
* **Technical Deep-Dive**: [What Is a Knitted Viscose Cleaning Cloth?](https://wondercloth.top/knowledge/what-is-viscose-cleaning-cloth.html)
