## 1. Overview & Scope

This specification standard defines the physical, mechanical, and surface properties of industrial and commercial-grade **plant-based knitted viscose (regenerated cellulose) cleaning fabrics**. Engineered specifically as a 100% biodegradable, zero-microplastic alternative to synthetic petroleum-based microfibers (polyester/polyamide), these textiles target heavy-duty degreasing, commercial kitchen sanitation, and optical wipe applications.

* **Primary Fiber Composition**: 100% Regenerated Wood/Plant Cellulose (Viscose) or Viscose/Cotton Blends
* **Knitting Technology**: High-gauge Circular Weft Knitting (丸編み機)
* **Key Competitive Differentiator**: Naturally hydrophilic, lipophilic grease release without chemical detergents, and thermal tolerance.
